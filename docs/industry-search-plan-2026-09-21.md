## Published architecture
Seven distinct industry pages plus an industry directory. Each contains equipment scope, three visible question/answer sections, call preparation, related trade services, and city links. Existing 16 city pages link to selected relevant industries. No mass-generated city × industry × symptom permutations.

1. Grocery/deli: meat slicer no-start, uneven cutting, warming display fridge.
2. Bakery: bread slicer stopping/jamming, oven temperature, maintenance scheduling.
3. Restaurant: oven no heat, dishwasher performance, multiple trades.
4. Laundry: washer not draining, dryer not heating, preventative maintenance.
5. Dry cleaner: mid-cycle stops, pressing equipment heat, service information.
6. Hospitality/property: hot water loss, shared laundry, ongoing maintenance.
7. Winery/farm market: retail refrigeration, seasonal maintenance, specialist machinery scope.

Do not present FAQs as a guarantee of Google rich results. Do not describe city associations as proof of demand. No invented case studies or repair histories were published. Earlier construction photos remain accurately labeled in the gallery but no longer lead the homepage.
